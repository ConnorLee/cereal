Three steps

  1. 1

    Install Cereal

    Open Cereal.dmg and drag Cereal to Applications. The first time, macOS asks you to allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

  2. 2

    Drop in your projects

    Drag project folders onto Cereal, or choose them. It reads nothing until you approve.

  3. 3

    Turn on the morning email

    Verify your address and add a free Resend key.

  • Read-only: never writes to your repos
  • Emails carry short summaries, never files or secrets
